Luis Espinosa Anke

NLP Specialist

I am a tech lead in Natural Language Processing at Envelop Risk Analytics and senior lecturer (~associate professor) at the CardiffNLP lab in Cardiff University. My research interests are computational semantics and computational lexicography. I explore how language models represent meaning at different granularities and what it takes for them to learn new words, new meanings and make common sense inferences.

In industry, I was previously head of machine learning at AMPLYFI, a Cardiff-based market intelligence startup, and before that, data scientist at Nationwide Building Society. I am a Fulbright scholar (2010-2011 cohort) and recipient of a laCaixa scholarship (2011 - 2013 cohort). I obtained my PhD at Pompeu Fabra University under the supervision of Horacio Saggion.
